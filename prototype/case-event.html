London Fashion Day

An international platform created to support emerging designers — founded, produced and operated by the studio.

FounderKostiantyn Lieontiev
RelationshipFounded

Platform

  1. What it is

    An international fashion platform created to give emerging designers a route to an audience.

  2. Studio role

    Founded, produced and operated by the studio. Not a commission — an ongoing platform the practice owns.

Programme, venue, participating designers, runway film, gallery, partners and credits are all defined in the model and deliberately absent: none has been supplied. On the live site they would not render at all.

An owned platform is not shaped like a one-night show. A show AK produced for a client leads with a date and a venue; a platform leads with what it is and what the studio does with it.
