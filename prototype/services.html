Six movements, one practice.

A brand is built across the whole cycle — idea, positioning, identity, image, experience, presence, visibility. We work in all of it, which is why the parts hold together.

Movements
  1. 01

    Strategy

    What it is. Deciding what the brand is, who it is for, and what it will not be — before anything is designed or bought.

    Who it is for. A founder starting out, a label that has outgrown its first position, or a person whose company is better known than they are.

    • Brand concept development
    • Brand positioning
    • Brand strategy
    • Brand relaunch
    • Repositioning
    • Brand philosophy
    • Identity foundations
    • Strategic guidance for growth
    Personal brand development
    • Personal brand strategy
    • Positioning
    • Identity
    • Communication strategy
    • Visual direction
    • Content direction
  2. 02

    Identity

    What it is. Giving the position a face that survives contact with the real world — at ad size before billboard size.

    Who it is for. Anyone whose brand looks different in every place it appears.

    • Brand identity development
    • Visual direction
    • Logo & identity design
    • Brand guidelines
    • Visual storytelling
    • Creative direction

    Creative direction is a studio capability, not a sub-service. It runs through identity, campaigns, photography, film, fashion, events and digital — and it is why work from different disciplines still looks like one hand.

  3. 03

    Image

    What it is. The pictures and the films — made by the studio, directed by the studio.

    Who it is for. A collection with nothing to show it in; a campaign that needs to exist before it can be promoted.

    • Creative direction
    • Photo campaigns
    • Promotional video production
    • Visual storytelling
    • Campaign production
  4. 04

    Experience

    What it is. What happens in a room — shows, launches, presentations, and the production behind them.

    Who it is for. A designer who needs a runway; a brand with something to put in front of an audience.

    • Brand presentations
    • Product launches
    • Creative events
    • Independent fashion shows
    • Fashion show production
    • Fashion-week-related production

    The studio does not only produce shows — it founded and operates two international fashion platforms.

  5. 05

    Digital

    What it is. Where the brand lives online: the site, the store, the feed.

    Who it is for. A brand whose website contradicts everything the identity says.

    • Website creation
    • Website development
    • Social media presence
    • Digital content production
    • Online brand positioning
  6. 06

    Visibility

    What it is. Getting the work seen — editorially and commercially. Two halves, weighted equally.

    Communication & PR
    • Marketing strategy
    • Brand communication
    • PR support
    • Campaign development
    Promotion & paid media
    • Digital advertising campaigns
    • Google promotion
    • YouTube promotion
    • Meta · Facebook · Instagram
    • Audience growth
    • Digital visibility
    • Engagement

    Advertising amplifies a brand system the other five movements built. It is not the practice, and on its own it is the most expensive way to hide a positioning problem.
