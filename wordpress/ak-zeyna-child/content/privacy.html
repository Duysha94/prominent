AK Brand Development Studio is an independent creative and strategic practice based in London. This page explains what happens to information you give us through this website.

What we collect

Only what you send us. If you complete the project brief on the contact page, we receive the name, email address, brand name, link and description you type into it. We do not ask for anything else, and there is nothing on this site that collects information about you in the background.

Why we hold it

To read your brief and reply to it. That is the whole purpose. We do not sell it, share it with anyone outside the studio, or add you to a mailing list you did not ask to join.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that turn into projects are kept for as long as the working relationship needs them, and for the period our accountants require afterwards. Enquiries that do not are deleted within twelve months.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Your choice of light or dark mode is stored in your own browser so the site remembers it on your next visit; it never leaves your device and we cannot read it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the studio and we will act on it. If you are not satisfied with how we respond, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version appears on this page.
