What the work required

Four movements, and what each one hands over.

  1. Strategy

    Decide what the brand is, who it is for, and what it will not be.

  2. Identity

    Give the position a face that survives contact with the real world.

  3. Production

    Make it exist — the campaign, the event, the show, the room.

  4. Presence

    Put it where people already are, and make it findable.
